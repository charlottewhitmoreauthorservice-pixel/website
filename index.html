Submission Guidelines

Everything You Need to Know Before Submitting

Every project at Mercyworld Read Network begins with a single email. This section tells you exactly what to include, what to expect, and how the process works from start to finish.

Required Information for Your Email

Your Full Name

Your legal name or pen name, exactly as you want it to appear professionally.

Book Title and Subtitle

The complete title including subtitle. If part of a series, indicate the series number.

Genre and Subgenre

Be as specific as possible. "Christian contemporary fiction with elements of suspense" is better than "Fiction."

Book Purchase Link

The direct URL where your book can be purchased. Amazon, your website, or any platform.

Your Marketing Goals

What do you want to achieve? Be specific about sales targets, visibility goals, and audience reach.

Your Timeline

When do you want the campaign to begin? Any launch dates or deadlines we should know about.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with self-published authors?

Yes. We work with self-published authors, traditionally published authors, and hybrid authors. Your publishing path does not matter. What matters is the quality of your book and your commitment to marketing it professionally.

Do you work with authors outside the United States?

Yes. We work with authors globally. Our process is entirely email-based, which means location is never a barrier. We have worked with authors in Europe, Africa, Asia, and South America.

How long does a typical campaign last?

It depends on the service. Visibility optimization takes 2 to 4 weeks. Launch campaigns run 4 to 8 weeks. Ongoing promotion runs for a minimum of 3 months. The Complete Marketing Package is customized to your needs.

How do I get pricing for your services?

We do not publish pricing on our website. Every author's book is different. To get pricing, email us at mercyworldreadnetwork@gmail.com with your book details. We will send you a detailed proposal within 48 to 72 hours.

What if I am not satisfied with the results?

We stand behind our work. If you are not satisfied with any aspect of your campaign, we will review the situation, document the issue, and work with you to resolve it. Our goal is to deliver results you are proud of.

Do you require a contract?

Yes. Every project begins with a formal project agreement sent via email. This agreement outlines the scope, deliverables, timeline, and terms. It protects both you and us. Everything is in writing.

Why email only? Can I call or message you?

Email creates a permanent record. Every conversation, every decision, every agreement is documented in writing. This means no misunderstandings, full accountability, and complete transparency on every project.
